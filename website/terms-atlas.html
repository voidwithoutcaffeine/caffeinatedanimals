Atlas terms

Last updated October 2, 2026. This is a starter policy for the utility bot.

1. What Atlas is

Atlas provides reminders, server info, custom replies, and support tickets. It does not replace Discord’s own support.

2. Tickets

Ticket text is visible to the member who opened it and to staff roles the server chooses. Do not put passwords, private addresses, or payment details in a ticket.

3. Data

Atlas may store reminder text until the reminder is sent, custom command text, and open ticket channel IDs. Closed tickets can be deleted by staff.

4. Custom commands

Staff are responsible for the text they save. Do not store anything that breaks Discord’s rules.

5. Limits

Reminders and tickets may be capped so one server cannot overload the bot. We may suspend a server that abuses commands.

6. Contact

Put your support server or email here before launch.
