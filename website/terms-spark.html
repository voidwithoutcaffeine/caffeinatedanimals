Spark terms

Last updated October 2, 2026. This is a starter policy for the community bot.

1. What Spark is

Spark posts welcomes, runs polls, tracks simple activity points, and reminds a server about events. It is for friendly community use.

2. Fair use

Do not use polls, levels, or welcome messages to trick people, spam channels, or single anyone out. Staff can turn features off at any time.

3. Data

Spark may store server settings, poll choices, and activity points tied to a Discord user ID inside that server. Points are not sold and are not used for ads.

4. Events and rewards

Any giveaway or reward is run by the server, not by Spark. Staff must follow Discord’s rules and local law for prizes.

5. Changes

Commands and point rules may be updated. Continued use after an update means you accept the new terms shown on this page.

6. Contact

Put your support server or email here before launch.
